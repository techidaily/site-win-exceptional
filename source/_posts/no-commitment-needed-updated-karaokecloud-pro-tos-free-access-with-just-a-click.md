## No Commitment Needed! Updated KaraokeCloud Pro TOS: Free Access with Just a Click

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/importanttermsofserviceupgrade-kcp-1.jpg?resize=573%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/importanttermsofserviceupgrade-kcp-1.jpg?fit=573%2C300&ssl=1 "importanttermsofserviceupgrade-kcp (1)")

_[**Karaoke Cloud Pro**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is the world’s first and only **professional karaoke subscription** for professional KJs and venues, and the terms of service agreement has just been updated._ 

Digitrax Entertainment are the creators and keepers of the Karaoke Cloud Pro subscription, and after almost 2 years of industry and subscription user [![karaoke-cloud-pro-logo](https://pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/karaoke-cloud-pro-logo.png)](https://pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/karaoke-cloud-pro-logo.png)feedback we’ve worked with them to revise the terms of service to allow users to start or cancel their professional KCP subsciption at any time. This means new KJs or seasoned vets can now try the service for a month, and if they don’t believe it’s a fit for their karaoke needs, they can easily cancel directly from the [**Manage My Subscription**](http://www.karaokelocker.com/change%5Facct.pl) link on the Karaoke Cloud Pro page at PCDJ.com 

Since many KJs and venues offer seasonal karaoke, PCDJ and Digitrax collectively concluded the 12 month locked-in contract was no longer optimal – and that more karaoke hosts would try the already proven karaoke subscription knowing so. The service is ever evolving, and currently includes over 10,000 high-quality karaoke tracks from some of the top karaoke manufacturers in the business:   _**DigiTrax Karaoke (formerly Chartbuster), SBI, Sunfly, Stage Stars, DK and Mr. Entertainer**_

The Karaoke Cloud Pro professional karaoke subscription works directly in PCDJ Karaoki, and users download the available karaoke tracks to their local drive for offline use (only need to connect to the net once a month to verify payment/subscription) and updates to the library are automatic – you simply retrieve the freshly added tunes when they are available through the KCP tab in our karaoke software.
